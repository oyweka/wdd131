Nairobi, Kenya

Nairobi is the capital city of Kenya. Its name comes from the Maasai word Enkare Nyrobi, meaning "place of cool water." It is the only capital city in the world with a national park. Some of the animals that can be viewed here include lions, elephants, giraffes, and even the endangered black rhino. It is surprisingly cool, despite being close to the equator.
