Understanding Rice Farming in Kenya

Site Name

Kenya Rice Farming

Kenya Rice Farming describes the purpose of this website. It focuses on rice production particularly in Kenya. It gives meaningful details regarding rice growing regions, practices followed, and challenges faced by rice farmers in the country.

Optional domain name: kenyaricefarming.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Kenya Rice Farming website is to make it easier for one to access valuable information regarding rice farming in Kenya. Those visiting the site will be able to see the major growing areas, the challenges farmers face in the cultivation process, and steps followed in growing rice.

The website will have information displayed in user-friendly manner to allow easier access to information by farmers, rice stakeholders, and anyone interested in learning about rice farming in Kenya.

Color Scheme

The color scheme matches the natural agricultural colors to reflect the environment in rice farming. White will provide clean content areas, dark green will be used for the header and main headings, while light green will be used for section backgrounds and accents, and dark gray will be used for body text.
